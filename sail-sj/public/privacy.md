# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Sail SJ ("Sail SJ," "we," "our," or "us") is an open-source web application example built using Scramjet and related technologies. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the Sail SJ hosted demo or run your own instance of the software.

**Project repository:** https://github.com/githubbozodude/sail-sj

**Hosted demo:** https://sail-sj-chi.vercel.app

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the Sail SJ project and its hosted demo to the extent they are operated by the project maintainer.

If you install, modify, fork, or independently host Sail SJ, the person or organization operating that instance is responsible for its own data practices. Their privacy policy and practices may differ from ours.

Third-party websites and services accessed through Sail SJ are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

## 3. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on how Sail SJ is hosted and configured, the following information may be processed:

- **Technical information:** IP addresses, browser type, operating system, timestamps, requested URLs, and server error information recorded by hosting providers or infrastructure.
- **Usage information:** Basic information about visits and application interactions, if analytics or diagnostic tools are enabled.
- **Browser storage:** Information stored locally by the application, including service-worker caches and other browser storage, depending on the implementation.
- **Proxy traffic:** URLs, request metadata, and content involved in requests you initiate through the application may pass through proxy infrastructure. The precise information processed depends on the configuration and destination.
- **Information you provide:** Information you voluntarily send through project contact channels, issue reports, or other communications.

We do not intend this list to imply that every category is collected in every deployment. Actual practices depend on the deployed code, hosting provider, and enabled integrations.

## 4. How Information May Be Used

Information may be processed when necessary to:

- Operate and maintain the application.
- Deliver requests made by users.
- Diagnose errors and improve reliability.
- Protect the application and its infrastructure against abuse.
- Respond to communications and security reports.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 5. Cookies and Local Storage

Sail SJ and its underlying technologies may use browser storage, including service-worker caches, to support application functionality.

Hosting providers or third-party integrations may also use cookies or similar technologies. The use of these technologies depends on the actual deployment.

You can manage or clear browser storage through your browser settings. Clearing storage may affect application functionality or remove locally cached data.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The hosted demo may depend on third-party hosting, networking, proxy, or other infrastructure services. Those providers may process technical information under their own policies and agreements.

Sail SJ does not control the privacy practices of independent websites you visit or third-party infrastructure providers.

Review the applicable providers' policies before using the hosted demo if you have concerns about data processing.

## 7. Data Retention

Retention depends on the information involved, the application's configuration, and the policies of the hosting and infrastructure providers.

Information may remain in server logs, diagnostic records, browser caches, or third-party systems for periods determined by the relevant operator. We will not claim a specific deletion schedule unless one has been established for the relevant system.

## 8. Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect the application and any information handled by its operators. However, no application, network, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid entering passwords, payment information, confidential documents, or other sensitive information into proxy applications unless they understand and trust the complete infrastructure handling their requests.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Sail SJ is not intended to encourage the collection of personal information from children. Operators of hosted instances should comply with the privacy laws applicable to their users and location.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

Requests concerning the hosted demo may be sent through the project's GitHub repository. Requests concerning an independently hosted instance must be directed to that instance's operator.

Some information may need to be retained for security, legal compliance, or other lawful purposes.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the project, its hosting arrangements, or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be published in the project repository.
